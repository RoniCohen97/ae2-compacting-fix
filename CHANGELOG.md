# Changelog

## 1.0.0+26.1.2

The 1.0.0 features, for Minecraft 26.1.2, NeoForge and AE2 26.1. It follows NeoForge's new transfer API, which replaces
item handlers.

## 1.0.0

The first release, for Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge and AE2 19.2.

- AE2 crafting plans count the tiers of a compacting drawer (nuggets, ingots, blocks...) as one stock, so a job no
  longer fails at launch with missing ingredients
- The plan screen shows what is missing for drawer items
- Storage Drawers and Functional Storage, directly or through their controllers
